# SOP — Incident Management

## Purpose
Restore IT services quickly while maintaining traceability and reducing repeat failures.

## Procedure
1. Log incident.
2. Determine impact and priority.
3. Assign owner.
4. Communicate to affected users when needed.
5. Troubleshoot / contain.
6. Restore service.
7. Validate business operation.
8. Document resolution.
9. Escalate recurring/high-impact incidents to problem review.
10. Track preventive actions.

## Major Incident
For critical outages, maintain a timeline and management communication until service is restored.
