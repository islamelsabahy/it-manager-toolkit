# IT Change Management

## Change Types
- Standard Change — pre-approved, low-risk, repeatable
- Normal Change — assessed and approved before implementation
- Emergency Change — urgent change required to restore service or reduce severe risk

## Required Planning
- Scope
- Business impact
- Technical impact
- Dependencies
- Implementation steps
- Validation plan
- Rollback plan
- Communications
- Required downtime

## Post-Implementation Review
- Was the change successful?
- Was rollback required?
- Were incidents introduced?
- Was downtime within plan?
- What should be improved?
