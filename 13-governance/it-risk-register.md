## Suggested Categories
- Availability
- Cybersecurity
- Vendor
- Compliance
- Financial
- Capacity
- Infrastructure
- Business Continuity
- Data
- People / Skills

## Rating Guidance
Use a simple 3x3 or 5x5 likelihood × impact matrix and define the scoring model in the organization.

## Minimum Controls
- Every High/Critical risk has an owner.
- Every risk has a business impact statement.
- Mitigation must be measurable.
- Accepted risks require explicit approval.
- Closed risks retain evidence and closure date.
