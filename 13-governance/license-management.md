## Controls
- Maintain a named owner for each subscription.
- Track purchased, assigned, and available seats.
- Review unused licenses before renewal.
- Record contract and renewal dates.
- Avoid unapproved software and shadow IT where policy applies.
- Reclaim licenses during offboarding.
- Validate licensing impact before major upgrades.

## Renewal Review
Before renewal, evaluate:
- Actual utilization
- Business criticality
- Alternative products
- Vendor performance
- Price increase
- Contract term
- Security / compliance requirements
- Consolidation opportunities
