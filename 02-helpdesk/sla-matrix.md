# Helpdesk SLA Matrix

| Priority | Example Impact | Target First Response | Target Resolution / Restore | Escalation |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| P1 Critical | Business-critical outage | 15 min | 4 hours / agreed restore target | Immediate IT Manager + vendor |
| P2 High | Department / key-user impact | 30 min | 8 business hours | IT Manager if at risk |
| P3 Medium | Individual operational issue | 2 business hours | 2 business days | Team lead |
| P4 Low | Request / minor issue | 4 business hours | 5 business days | Standard |

> SLA targets are examples. Adapt to business hours, staffing, contracts, and service criticality.
