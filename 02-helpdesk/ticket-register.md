## Priority Model

### P1 — Critical
Major business outage, severe security event, or critical service unavailable.

### P2 — High
Significant user/team impact with limited workaround.

### P3 — Medium
Normal operational issue with workaround or limited impact.

### P4 — Low
Request, minor issue, improvement, or information request.
