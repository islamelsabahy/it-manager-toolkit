# IT Business Continuity Checklist

## Critical Services
- [ ] Critical business services identified
- [ ] Business owners confirmed
- [ ] Service dependencies documented
- [ ] RTO defined
- [ ] RPO defined

## People
- [ ] Primary and backup IT contacts defined
- [ ] Vendor escalation contacts current
- [ ] Emergency communication channel available
- [ ] Key-person dependency risks reviewed

## Technology
- [ ] Backup connectivity considered
- [ ] Critical configuration backups available
- [ ] Remote access capability reviewed
- [ ] Spare/loan devices considered
- [ ] Power/UPS/generator dependencies understood

## Data
- [ ] Critical datasets identified
- [ ] Backup frequency aligned to RPO
- [ ] Restore testing performed
- [ ] Off-site/cloud copy considered according to risk

## Sites
- [ ] Alternate-work arrangements considered
- [ ] Critical branch/site dependencies documented
- [ ] Physical access requirements documented

## Testing
- [ ] Tabletop scenario completed
- [ ] Recovery actions tested where practical
- [ ] Gaps logged as risks/actions
- [ ] Management receives test summary
