# IT Risk Heatmap

## Scoring Model

### Likelihood
| Score | Meaning |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Rare |
| 2 | Unlikely |
| 3 | Possible |
| 4 | Likely |
| 5 | Almost Certain |

### Impact
| Score | Meaning |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Insignificant |
| 2 | Minor |
| 3 | Moderate |
| 4 | Major |
| 5 | Severe / Critical |

## Risk Score

`Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact`

## Rating
| Score | Rating | Expected Action |
|---:|---|---|
| 1–4 | Low | Accept / monitor |
| 5–9 | Medium | Planned mitigation |
| 10–16 | High | Active mitigation + management visibility |
| 17–25 | Critical | Immediate action / executive escalation |
