# IT KPI Catalog

| KPI | Definition | Formula / Measurement | Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| SLA Compliance % | Tickets resolved within agreed SLA | SLA-compliant tickets / SLA tickets × 100 | Monthly |
| MTTR | Average time to restore/resolve | Sum resolution time / resolved incidents | Monthly |
| Ticket Backlog | Open unresolved tickets | Count open tickets | Weekly |
| Backup Success % | Successful scheduled backup jobs | Successful jobs / scheduled jobs × 100 | Weekly |
| Patch Compliance % | In-scope devices compliant with patch baseline | Compliant devices / in-scope devices × 100 | Monthly |
| Asset Accuracy % | Assets verified against register | Verified assets / sampled assets × 100 | Quarterly |
| Availability % | Service uptime | Uptime / total scheduled time × 100 | Monthly |
| Budget Variance % | Difference between actual and budget | (Actual - Budget) / Budget × 100 | Monthly |
| Vendor SLA % | Vendor SLA compliance | Met SLA events / total measured events × 100 | Monthly |
